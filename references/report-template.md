## Confidence Levels

| Level | Meaning | When to Use |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **High** | Data from authoritative source (government DB, official filings) | Registration data, capital, legal status |
| **Medium** | Data from reliable secondary source (HR platforms, trade directories) | Employee count, business description |
| **Low** | Inferred from indirect signals or limited data | Revenue estimate, budget capacity |

## Report Length Guidelines

- **Quick check** (user just wants basic info): Sections 1-2 only, ~200 words
- **Standard** (pre-negotiation): Sections 1-6, ~500 words
- **Full due diligence** (before large contract): All sections, ~800-1000 words
